HOUSE OF J&S COMPUTER TRAINING CENTER
SCRATCH 3.0 ACADEMY — 15 EXAM SET EDITION

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the js_scratch_course folder.
3. Double-click index.html in Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari.
4. Internet is recommended for Google Fonts and the official Scratch editor link.

COURSE CONTENT
- 38 guided lessons across 5 levels: Beginner, Elementary, Intermediate, Advanced and Projects.
- English and Myanmar explanations in every lesson.
- Search and level filters for lessons.
- Lesson knowledge checks and progress tracking saved in the current browser.
- Enlarged typography and responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile.

CREATIVE PRACTICE STUDIO
- 28 extra step-by-step practice activities.
- Shape drawing: lines, square, triangle, pentagon, hexagon, octagon, star, spiral, T shape, I shape, house and geometric patterns.
- Small games: Apple Catcher, Click the Target, Maze Escape, Space Shooter, Paddle & Ball and Reaction Time.
- Projects: Animated Name Card, Interactive Story, Quiz Show, Virtual Pet, Drawing App and Platform Jumper.
- Challenges include polygon angles, debugging, nested loops and reusable custom polygon blocks.
- Each activity includes block recipes, ordered steps, expected results, a challenge and a hint.

EXAMINATION SYSTEM
- 15 complete topic-focused examination sets.
- 20 questions per set, 100 marks and a 30-minute timer.
- 300 question slots across the 15 papers; core concepts are intentionally reinforced across more than one set.
- Topics: Scratch Foundations; Events & Motion; Looks, Costumes & Sound; Control & Loops; Pen & Shape Drawing; Operators & Decisions; Sensing & Interaction; Variables, Score & Timers; Lists & Data; Broadcasts & Clones; My Blocks & Procedures; Animation & Sound Design; Maze & Platformer Logic; Debugging & Project Quality; Comprehensive Final Challenge.
- Question formats: multiple choice, true/false, fill in the blanks, arrange Scratch blocks in order, and matching.
- The 30-minute timer automatically submits an unfinished paper when time expires.
- After submission, students can review their responses, correct answers and explanations.
- Best percentage, attempt status and attempt count for each set are saved in the current browser.
- Quick Mixed Practice and a Block Order Challenge are also available.

ABOUT SCRATCH BLOCK VISUALS
The site presents Scratch block names in colour-coded visual stacks for learning. These are teaching representations, not official Scratch screenshots or a live embedded editor. Recreate and run scripts in the official Scratch 3.0 editor: https://scratch.mit.edu/projects/editor/

PROGRESS & LIMITATIONS
Progress and exam scores are stored locally in the current browser using localStorage. This static front-end portal does not include online student accounts, a cloud database, teacher account permissions or secure proctoring. Clearing browser data or changing devices may remove locally saved results.

IMPORTANT AUTHENTICATION LIMITATION
This is a static/offline-friendly front-end classroom sign-in gate. The password is included in the website code and can be inspected by someone who has the files. There is no server-side authentication, encrypted password storage, online student account database or teacher admin system. Do not use this as a security boundary for private student records. A production multi-student login system needs a backend, database and properly hashed passwords.
